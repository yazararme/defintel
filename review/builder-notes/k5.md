of 150–209 characters. The prompt limits in `k5-prompt.md` fix all nine, measured in a
shortened browser-only copy, locally and in the CI worst case. **The alarm day, 14 Sep, cannot

summary heading at 373px or lower (the threshold is 300). That needs a decision, set out
below. I changed no layout, because no layout change I could make passes on its own.

## The alarm day (14 Sep): why no layout change passes

In 14 Sep's case, the band (`.alarmbar`) and the whole ALARMLAR section sit between the title
and the summary. The section is 431px: its heading plus a 12-line paragraph. That paragraph
also carries the development's explanation ("Önemli, çünkü …"), which Rev 6 gives to the
development, not to ALARMLAR.

I tested these variants in the browser only, with CSS injected and text shortened to the
limits:

| variant | h2 / item 4 local | CI worst |
|---|---|---|
| as published | 840 / 1423 | 840 / 1449 |
| headline and item limits only | 808 / 1234 | 808 / 1260 |
| + band ≤ 2 lines, ALARMLAR paragraph ≤ 2 lines | 495 / 920 | 495 / 947 |
| + chip strip after the summary on alarm days, band tightened (6px padding, 8px margin) | 424 / 849 | 424 / 876 |
| **same, with band and ALARMLAR paragraph each 1 line (the extreme)** | **373** / 799 | 373 / 825 |
| ALARMLAR section after the summary, band stays above the title, chips after the summary, band tightened, band ≤ 2 lines + limits | **284 / 710** | **284 / 736** |
| same, but band text unlimited (3 lines) | 306 / 731 | 306 / 758 |
| same layout, no text limits | 338 / 921 | 338 / 947 |

The arithmetic: 300px − 98 (masthead and daybar) − 8 = 194px for the headline, the chip
strip and the alarm. A 3-line headline takes 105px and the chip strip 59px, which leaves 30px.
A one-line band alone needs about 44px, and the ALARMLAR heading alone needs about 42px before
its first line. So **while the ALARMLAR section stays above the summary, no alarm day can
pass**, whatever the text length. Only one geometry passes: the band (the alarm's name and
deadline, in alarm colour) stays above the title, and the ALARMLAR prose follows the summary.
That runs into the brief's "özetin altına alma" if that phrase means the section and not only
the band. Rev 6 adds another limit: the move must apply at every width. If it applied only
below 700 or 920px, rotating a tablet would change the order of the text block.

**Decision needed (orchestrator or customer):**
- **(a)** Accept "the band is the alarm above the summary". ALARMLAR's prose then moves after
  the summary at every width. The chip strip moves after the summary on alarm days at ≤700px
  (chrome only). `alarm_title` gets a limit of ≤ 90 characters (2 lines).
  Estimate: 284 / 710, CI 284 / 736.
- **(b)** Keep ALARMLAR above the summary, and accept that İLK-EKRAN fires on every alarm day.
  The customer has already ruled this out ("muafiyet yok").

I implemented neither, because (a) goes against the brief's letter.
